# site-sundae-campaign

The campaign overview for **Sundae** (prepared by SHAI): the Oct. 8, 2026, Private Dinner &
Dialogue in the Courtyard at Shade Hotel in Manhattan Beach, and the always-on Sundae Membership
(franchise) growth engine.

- `/` overview · `/creative` 10 images, 14 flyers (+PDF), 8 videos, 8 Reels · `/captions` 16 captions
- `/plans/*` 8 plans (event organic/paid, filming, membership organic/paid, SHAI playbook, action plan, compliance)
- `/projections` scenario charts + calculator (data: `src/content/projections.json`, generated by
  `clients/sundae/marketing/projections/model.py` in the studio repo)
- Next.js 16 · Tailwind v4 · React Three Fiber (lead-funnel hero) · GSAP + Lenis
- Styled to Sundae's Creative Guidelines (`clients/sundae/BRAND-SPEC.md` in the studio repo): Merriweather
  headlines and Lato everywhere else, palette tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, chart colors in
  `src/content/palette.ts` (blue, slate, ink; red is an accent and never text), AP style in all copy.
- `public/media/ai-start-frames/` are the Shade Courtyard frames used to generate the AI B-roll.
- `robots: noindex` (review build).
